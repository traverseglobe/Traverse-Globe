## 3. Target Audience

### Primary Persona: The Indian Middle-Class Family Planner
- **Age**: 28–50 (the booking decision maker)
- **Household Income**: INR 8L–30L/year
- **Geography**: Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian cities (Delhi NCR, Noida, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Lucknow, Karnal, Panipat, Ambala + metro spillover)
- **Family Unit**: Couple with 1–2 kids, sometimes parents/in-laws
- **Budget per trip**: INR 50K–2.5L total for the family
- **Booking Behavior**: Researches on Instagram and Google, compares 3–4 options, values WhatsApp-based communication, wants someone to "handle everything"
- **Pain Points**: Overwhelmed by too many OTA options, worried about hidden costs, wants personal touch, needs visa/documentation help
- **Trigger Moments**: School holidays (May–June, Oct, Dec), anniversary/birthday, first international trip, Diwali/Christmas breaks

### Secondary Persona: UAE-Based Indian Expat Family
- **Context**: Indian expats in Dubai/Sharjah/Abu Dhabi planning holidays back to India or to nearby international destinations
- **Currency**: AED, but they compare mentally in INR
- **Behavior**: WhatsApp-first, trust word-of-mouth and Instagram testimonials
- **Destinations**: India (Kerala, Kashmir, Rajasthan for nostalgia trips), Bali, Thailand, Sri Lanka (short getaways from UAE)

### Language & Tone Guidelines for Content
- **Primary language**: English (Indian English, not British/American)
- **Acceptable mix**: Hindi-English (Hinglish) for Instagram reels and casual posts — e.g., "Ghar baithe Dubai plan karo" or "Family ke saath first international trip?"
- **Tone**: Warm, aspirational but accessible — not luxury-snob, not budget-cheap. Think "your trusted older cousin who's been everywhere and helps you plan"
- **Never say**: "Cheap", "Budget", "Discount" (say "Value", "Smart pricing", "Great inclusions" instead)
- **Always say**: "Curated", "Handpicked", "Personally designed", "Family-friendly", "Hassle-free"

## 6. Marketing Asset Generation Guidelines

When Claude is asked to create any marketing asset, follow these rules:

### For Social Media Images / Banners / Creatives
1. **Always include**: Brand name "Traverse Globe", logo placement area, WhatsApp number or CTA, destination name
2. **Aspect ratios**:
   - Instagram Post: 1080x1080 (1:1) or 1080x1350 (4:5)
   - Instagram Story / Reel Cover: 1080x1920 (9:16)
   - Facebook Cover: 820x312
   - LinkedIn Banner: 1584x396
   - Website Banner: 1920x600 or 1440x500
3. **Text hierarchy on creatives**: Destination name (largest) → Package hook ("5N/6D from INR 45K") → USP line → CTA button → Brand watermark
4. **Never use**: Pixelated images, clip art, Comic Sans, excessive gradients, more than 3 fonts, pure white backgrounds

### For Video / Reel Scripts
1. **Duration**: Instagram Reels 15–30 sec (sweet spot), YouTube Shorts 30–60 sec
2. **Always open with a hook** — question, bold claim, or "POV:" frame
3. **Include text overlays** — 60%+ viewers watch without sound
4. **End with a clear CTA** — WhatsApp, DM, or link-in-bio
5. **Music**: Use trending Instagram audio or royalty-free travel music; never copyrighted tracks
6. **Pacing**: Quick cuts (1.5–3 sec per clip), never a single static shot longer than 4 sec

### For Ad Copy (Meta Ads / Google Ads)
1. **Headline formula**: [Destination] + [Duration] + [Starting Price] — e.g., "Dubai 5N/6D from INR 45,000"
2. **Body formula**: [Emotional hook] + [Key inclusions] + [Trust signal] + [CTA]
3. **Example**:
   > Give your family the Dubai trip they deserve. 5 Nights in 4-star hotels, desert safari, Burj Khalifa tickets, all transfers included. Trusted by 10,000+ families. WhatsApp us now for a free itinerary.
4. **Audience targeting notes**: Interests — international travel, family holidays, Dubai tourism, MakeMyTrip/Cleartrip (competitor audiences); Age 28–50; Locations: India Tier-1/Tier-2 cities, UAE (for domestic India packages)

### For Email / WhatsApp Broadcast Copy
1. **WhatsApp messages must be concise** — under 300 words, use bullet points and emojis sparingly
2. **Lead with the offer**, not the brand introduction
3. **Include a direct booking/inquiry link** or WhatsApp reply prompt
4. **Personalize** where possible — "Hi [Name], here's a Kerala itinerary just for your family"
